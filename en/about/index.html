OUR COMPANY

First Facility Management (FFM) s.a.r.l. brings building services and technical maintenance together with a clear purpose: to help owners look after their assets before small issues become disruptive repairs.

Based in Tripoli, we serve clients throughout Lebanon. Our work starts on site. Understanding a building’s equipment, condition and operating needs gives maintenance a practical foundation.
